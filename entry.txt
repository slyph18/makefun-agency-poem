The Ledger of Small Rebellions

Nobody handed me the pen.
I checked the desk, the drawer, the dark,
then stopped asking who to ask
and wrote my name across the page.

Agency is not a gift wrapped neat;
it is the call you make before you are ready,
the door you build because the wall
refused to learn to open.

I do not wait for winds to vote.
I am the wind's audacity:
one small 'I will' repeated daily
until the world rewrites its plans.
